MiniMax Music 3 just landed in [llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/32435).

So I made 10 Christmas songs on my 2x RTX 5070 Ti, fully local:

90 s of music in 127 s per song\
18.0 GiB of VRAM with Q8_0 (BF16 doesn't fit)\
about 10.6 Wh per song, 21 min for all 10

Seed 42, first take, no cherry-picking. Prompts and lyrics by Claude.

Gotta say, way better than my first try this morning. The one in the video is the 1950s big band swing.

## Setup

**Model.** MiniMax Music 3, [Q8_0 GGUF](https://huggingface.co/ggml-org/MiniMax-Music3-GGUF) (ggml-org), mmproj in BF16. The BF16 weights did not fit in 2 x 16 GB.

**Runtime.** llama-tts from llama.cpp, upstream master (edec462, 18 Nov 2026), split across both GPUs.

**Machine.** 2x RTX 5070 Ti 16 GB, Intel Core Ultra 7 270K Plus, 64 GB RAM, Ubuntu 26.04.

## Generation

**Speed.** 90 s of stereo audio (44.1 kHz) per song in 127 s: 1.41 s of compute per second of music.

**Memory and power.** Peak VRAM 18.0 GiB (12.6 GiB on GPU 0, 5.4 GiB on GPU 1). GPU 0 does most of the work: about 236 W against 57 W on GPU 1.

**Energy.** About 10.6 Wh of GPU energy per song; 21 minutes and 106 Wh for all ten.

**Sampling.** Seed 42, first take, no retries.
